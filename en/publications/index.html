Federico Toledo updated on November 22, 2024

Publications

I like to write and to help others write. My blog was where I practiced the writing habit, sharing things about testing, leadership and related topics. I have also contributed to publications in other places, so here is the list.

Books

You can download my book Introducción a las Pruebas de Sistemas de Información (Introduction to Information Systems Testing, in Spanish), in its third edition published in 2024. The first edition from 2014 is still available here.

In 2020 I contributed a chapter to the book Around the world with 80 software testers, by Viv Richards.

In 2016 we published the automation chapter of my book, translated into English with some updates and improvements, as an ebook: A complete introduction to Functional Test Automation

Also as an ebook, we published The ultimate guide to continuous testing.

PhD thesis

I did my PhD at Universidad de Castilla-La Mancha, in Ciudad Real, Spain, between 2011 and 2014, with a scholarship from ANII.

The thesis is published in the Ruidera repository under the title “Mandinga: methodology for automation testing integrating functional and non-functional aspects”.
