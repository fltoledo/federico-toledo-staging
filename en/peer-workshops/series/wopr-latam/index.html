WOPR Latam

Performance, observability and reliability (SRE). The Latin American edition of WOPR, running since 2003.

WOPR was first held in 2003 in New York and has made major contributions to the performance testing, observability and SRE community ever since. It has been hosted by companies such as Google, Microsoft, Facebook, Salesforce, eBay and BlazeMeter across the United States, Canada and Europe.

In 2022 it came to Latin America for the first time, hosted by Abstracta in Montevideo. In 2023 WOPR Latam was born: the first peer workshop run entirely in Spanish, organized by Federico Toledo and Andrei Guchín with the support of Eric Proegler.
