Real experiences, discussed among peers.

We bring software professionals together to share what they lived through in their projects, and to learn in depth from what others lived through. These are not typical conference talks, nor a typical workshop: it is a space to learn from experience reports, with the chance to ask questions without limits, in a safe space.

For companies

The same, with your teams

In a large organization, each team learns on its own what another team already solved. We bring the format to your company and run the whole cycle, so that knowledge flows and turns into action.

How it works

  1. A call opens with the theme, date and city of the edition.
  2. You submit an experience report: something you lived first-hand that is worth discussing.
  3. About 20 people are selected, looking for the best stories and as much diversity as possible.
  4. The strongest reports get mentoring, so speakers prepare a presentation aligned with what the session needs to get the most out of it, and sharpen their speaking and presentation skills along the way.
  5. Three days of workshop: reports are presented and the group asks, challenges and learns.

What makes a peer workshop different

  • Nobody comes to sell: you talk about what you did and saw.
  • 30-minute presentations, discussions with no time limit.
  • K-Card facilitation keeps the conversation flowing.
  • A safe space: what is confidential stays in the room.
  • Free for participants, thanks to the host organizations.
