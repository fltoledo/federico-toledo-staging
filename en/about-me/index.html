About me

My purpose is closely aligned with that of my company: to promote quality. The quality of software, the quality of our work, the quality of relationships within the team and with clients, the quality of life. I am proud of what I do, and that is why I like to talk about it, to spread it and multiply it (this is what motivates me to write, give talks, write books, and so on).

In short:

  • I define my role as a Quality Engineer. I am one of the founders of Abstracta, where I worked on creating GXtest, Apptim (formerly known as Monkop) and Abstracta Academy.
  • I am a Computer Engineer from Universidad de la República in Uruguay, and I hold a PhD in #testing from Universidad de Castilla-La Mancha in Spain, where I lived for three years under the supervision of Macario Polo and Beatriz Pérez, in the Alarcos group led by Mario Piattini.
  • I have been working on and researching performance testing, test automation and functional testing since 2005.
  • I am a fan of productivity with purpose, with a focus on personal organization, agile methodologies and DevOps.
  • In recent years I have been very excited about everything related to the latest in generative artificial intelligence.
  • I wrote what I consider to be the first practical book on software testing in Spanish, with a new edition in 2024, and I contribute to several blogs. I have written on this site and on the Abstracta blog in English and in Spanish.
  • I was one of the founders of NahualUY, I was an organizer of the TestingUY conference for several years, and of meetups such as Abstracta Tech Talks, TestingUy, Nahual and Softskills.
  • Since 2022 I have focused on organizing Quality Sense Conf together with WOPR Latam, taking the conference and the workshop to different countries in Latin America.
  • To this day I collaborate on testing research and teaching at several universities (UdelaR, UCUDAL and UDE in Uruguay, UCLM in Spain, CNR in Italy).

I am truly committed to testing, and passionate about agile methodologies, DevOps and artificial intelligence.

This is me, in case you spot my face in some forum, community or blog:

I like to spread the word, write, speak, discuss and share about anything related to quality or testing. I like quality. I like to observe and give feedback in general, not only about software.

My academic experience taught me that I should not go beyond teaching. Academic research is not for me; I prefer the research done within companies. I need to see things working.

Although I was born in Montevideo (Uruguay), I lived until I was 18 near Atlántida, to the north, in a semi-rural area. Then I moved to the capital to study, and that is where I put down roots. I lived for 3 years in Ciudad Real (Spain) and 3 months in Pisa (Italy) while studying for my PhD. Studying and living in another country is an experience I recommend to everyone; I feel it helped me grow enormously. At the beginning of 2014 I returned to Montevideo, and although I miss my friends and the days in Castilla-La Mancha and Tuscany, this is the city where I want to live. At the end of 2017 I married the woman I love, Ale, who complements me and makes me grow and learn every day. In 2019 we moved to Berkeley, California, in the United States. In 2022 we came back, and today we live in El Pinar with our little Sofía 😍

I have taken on personal projects, such as building a house in Villa Serrana together with Ale (oh, we have a certificate that says we are steel frame installation technicians). We built it ourselves, doing a lot of hands-on work. It taught us a lot about something very different from what we usually do day to day. I highly recommend embarking on projects like this.

I enjoy spending time with family and friends, and outdoor activities such as running, cycling, fishing, kayaking or backpacking. I have always liked to travel, and I especially love combining work trips with getting to know new places or people, and sharing time with friends and family.

In this lovely interview from early 2022 (in Spanish) you can learn more about me.
