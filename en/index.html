Hi, I'm Federico 👋

I promote quality: in software, in teams and in the testing community in Latin America.

  • 🧪 Quality engineer: performance, test automation and testing strategy since 2005.
  • 🚀 Co-founder and CQO at Abstracta. We're always hiring: work with us.
  • 🎤 247 talks and workshops. Next: ¿Cómo hacer que la IA sea una oportunidad para todos? (nov 2026).
  • 📚 Author of Introducción a las Pruebas de Sistemas de Información.
  • 🤝 I organize Quality Sense Conf and peer workshops such as WOPR Latam and TAPIA.
  • ✍️ These days I write on the Abstracta blog. My blog from 2016 to 2025 is now an archive (in Spanish).

Timeline

  1. 2002–2007 Computer Engineering at Universidad de la República (UdelaR), Uruguay.
  2. 2005 My first job as a tester.
  3. 2006 First talks on performance testing, in Montevideo.
  4. 2008 I co-found Abstracta.
  5. 2010 I leave my job as an employee to focus on Abstracta and research.
  6. 2011–2014 PhD in software testing at Universidad de Castilla-La Mancha, Spain.
  7. 2013 Research internship at CNR (Consiglio Nazionale delle Ricerche) in Pisa, Italy.
  8. 2014 I publish Introducción a las Pruebas de Sistemas de Información.
  9. 2014–2019 I co-found Nahual Uruguay and train young people in testing to help them enter the workforce.
  10. 2015 I co-found Abstracta Academy.
  11. 2015–2019 I co-organize TestingUY, one of the first testing conferences in Uruguay.
  12. 2017 💍 I marry Ale.
  13. 2019 🏡 We build our getaway home in Villa Serrana.
  14. 2019–2022 I live in California, growing Abstracta in the United States.
  15. 2020 I launch the Quality Sense Podcast.
  16. 2022 I bring WOPR to Uruguay (its first time outside Europe and the United States), and with it comes the first Quality Sense Conf.
  17. 2023 I step down as COO and become Chief Quality Officer at Abstracta.
  18. 2023 First WOPR Latam, the peer workshop on performance, in Spanish.
  19. 2023 👶 Sofi is born.
  20. 2024 Third edition of the book, which we also translated into English.
  21. 2024–2026 Board member of CUTI, the Uruguayan Chamber of Information Technologies.
  22. 2026 First TAPIA, the peer workshop on testing and artificial intelligence.
  23. 2026 🏗️ We are building our home in El Pinar.
